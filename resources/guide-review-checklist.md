# Guide Review Checklist

Use this before treating a guide as publication-ready.

## Reader Value

- Reader and task are clear.
- The guide teaches a repeatable operating pattern.
- Advice is concrete enough to apply.
- Examples are portable.

## Evidence

- Current product claims have source links and verification dates.
- Internal or personal usage has been converted into public-safe patterns.
- Checks or proof points are included.
- Failure modes are named.

## Safety

- No local paths, usernames, hostnames, IDs, tokens, client names, or internal-only terminology.
- Screenshots, logs, and examples are sanitized.
- The guide does not imply access to private systems.

## Fit

- The guide belongs in `guides/`, not `resources/`.
- Detailed process notes are moved to `docs/`.
- The root README links only to the strongest entry points.
